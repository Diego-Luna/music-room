Privacy Policy

Educational Project: This application was built solely for academic and learning purposes in the context of the "Music Room" project at 42 school. It is non-profit, does not trade user data, and does not collect information for commercial purposes.

1. Information We Collect

Our application requests access to basic data from your Facebook account via Facebook Login:

  • Email address: Used exclusively to create a unique user identifier in our database and allow you to save your custom settings and playlists.
  • Public profile (Name and Profile Picture): Used solely to display your name and profile picture within the app to personalize your interface.

2. Use of Information

All authentication data retrieved through Facebook Login is stored securely and used only for core application features (signing in, voting for songs in real-time music rooms, and managing shared playlists). We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to any third-party organizations.

3. Data Security

We implement standard security measures to protect your information. Since this is a testing environment, no sensitive information (such as passwords of social networks or financial details) is ever requested or stored.

User Data Deletion Instructions

In accordance with Meta Platform terms and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we provide clear instructions for users to request the deletion of their information associated with Music Room.

How to delete your data

You can request the deletion of your account data through either of the following methods:

Method A: Remove the app via Facebook Settings

  1. Go to your Facebook Profile's Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click on Settings.
  3. Scroll down and select Apps and Websites.
  4. Find the Music Room application and click Remove.

Method B: Direct Deletion Request

If you want us to manually delete all your records (email, display name, and avatar URL) from the development database, please send an email with the subject "User Data Deletion Request - Music Room" to the project developer:

Contact Email: diegofcolunalopez@hotmail.com
Your request will be processed within 48 hours.
